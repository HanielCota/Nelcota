# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in Nelcota a respectful, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected behavior includes empathy, respect for differing experiences, constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes and prioritizing the community's
well-being. Discuss ideas and technical evidence without attacking people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language
and imagery, unwanted sexual attention, insults, threats, trolling, personal
attacks, sharing another person's private information without consent, and other
behavior inappropriate in a professional community. Retaliation against a report
or participation in an investigation is also unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibilities

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
Discussions and project events, and when someone represents the project publicly.
Maintainers explain and enforce these standards. They may remove or edit
contributions that violate them and restrict participation, explaining the
decision when appropriate. Technical disagreement alone is not a violation.

## Reporting and privacy

Report conduct incidents privately to **financeiro@hanielfialho.com**. Include
the relevant links, dates, what happened and any immediate safety concerns.
Do not publish personal details in an issue. Maintainers handle reports with
discretion, share information only as needed for assessment/enforcement and
avoid promising absolute confidentiality where disclosure is necessary.

If a report involves a maintainer, identify the conflict so another uninvolved
maintainer can assess it where available. For threats or platform abuse, you
may also use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools. Security vulnerabilities
use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers consider severity, impact, pattern of behavior and willingness to
correct it. Serious cases can skip earlier steps:

1. **Correction:** explain the problem privately and request a change or apology.
2. **Warning:** state consequences of repetition and any temporary interaction restrictions.
3. **Temporary ban:** restrict participation for a specified period following serious or repeated violations; no contact with affected people during that period.
4. **Permanent ban:** remove participation for sustained harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats or other severe violations.

Communicate restrictions and their duration directly when feasible. You may
request reconsideration through the reporting address with new relevant
information; an uninvolved maintainer reviews it where available. Do not use
public threads to pressure reporters or disclose private evidence.
